Working paper 01 · framework and method

The substance of an economy is what it can reproduce.

Output metrics answer one question well. They answer a different question badly, and that second question is the one that decides whether a society holds together. This is a framework for asking not only how much capability a society has, but where it came from and whether it would survive the withdrawal of its current source.

01

GDP is a flow metric answering a stock question

This is not a complaint about accuracy. GDP is a well-built measure of market production on a territory during a period. The problem is that a flow has been assigned a stock question, and the assignment is so routine that the mismatch is no longer noticed.

Four structural biases, not four errors It counts only what is priced. It is gross rather than net. It is territorial rather than residential. And it is indifferent to composition, so defensive expenditure registers as growth.

02

Three ways a capability can be false

Existing indices measure the level of capability. Two further questions turn a scoreboard into a diagnostic. A capability that is purchased, subsidised or staged scores high on level and low on substance.

L

Level

Is it adequate?

The conventional question, and the only one the existing instruments ask. Measured by direct assessment wherever one exists: learning poverty rather than years of schooling, effective coverage rather than beds.

E

Endogeneity

Is it produced here?

Or is it imported, rented or donor-supplied? Expatriate clinicians, imported active pharmaceutical ingredients, a foreign examination board, a remittance-financed household budget.

D

Durability

Would it survive?

Through a funding withdrawal, a political transition, a price spike, a programme being renamed. Statutory entitlement survives; a named scheme does not.

Domain substance

S = L0.50 · E0.25 · D0.25

A weighted geometric mean, because the axes are not substitutes. A domain fully present but entirely imported is not two-thirds of a capability — it is a contingent one, and the product form says so.

Composite, with a binding-constraint penalty

SI = G · [ (1−γ) + γ · mind Sd ]

G is the geometric mean across domains. γ sets how far the weakest domain drags the total. At γ = 0 this is the HDI rule; at γ = 1 a failed domain zeroes the score. Default γ = 0.5.

03

Four positions, and four different policy problems

Plotting level against durability produces four positions. Two jurisdictions can carry the same composite score and require opposite policies — which is why the composite should never be published without its diagnostic.

Solid — present and reproduced Staged — reach without institution Purchased — produced elsewhere Latent or hollow Bubble area ∝ GDP per capita index · synthetic data

Why the quadrant is the product, not the rank A single published rank under a single aggregation rule is a claim the data cannot support. The quadrant names the problem: convert reach into institutions, or build domestic production of the capability, or accept a contingent position and price it.

04

Six domains a society must reproduce

Each is stated with the conventional proxy, why that proxy fails, the substance indicators, the two tests, and the known defect in the underlying data. A seventh, ecological reproduction, is deliberately carried as a satellite account rather than folded into the score.

D1 Foundational competence Can an ordinary adult read, count and reason well enough to act in the world?

What gets measured instead

Mean years of schooling; gross enrolment; university rankings

Why that fails

Years of schooling count time served, not capability acquired. Enrolment measures presence in a building. Neither detects a child who has sat through eight grades without learning to read a paragraph.

Substance indicators

  • Learning poverty: share of 10-year-olds below minimum reading proficiency
  • Functional numeracy at age 15 on an applied task (unitary method, dosage, interest)
  • Adult literacy measured by direct assessment, not self-report
  • Unsubsidised learning rate: share at proficiency without paid tutoring
  • Teacher time-on-task from direct classroom observation

The two tests

Endogeneity. Share of teachers trained in domestically financed institutions; Curriculum and examination authority held domestically; Reliance on foreign school boards or imported textbooks.

Durability. Learning retained after a six-month interruption in instruction; Share of proficiency that survives the withdrawal of household tuition spending; Assessment cadence: is the country measuring itself, or waiting for a donor survey?.

Known data defectInternational assessments are voluntary, so the countries that participate are not a random sample. Learning poverty imputes regional values for non-reporting countries. Citizen-led surveys such as ASER are rural-only and district-sampled.
D2 Health capability Does illness impoverish a household, and would care survive without the current programme?

What gets measured instead

Life expectancy; health spending as a share of GDP; hospital beds per 1,000

Why that fails

Life expectancy is a lagging stock that moves slowly and hides distribution. Spending measures input, not access. Beds measure capacity to admit, not capacity to cure, and say nothing about who pays.

Substance indicators

  • UHC service coverage index (tracer services, geometric mean across four categories)
  • Catastrophic out-of-pocket spending: share of households above 10% of budget
  • Impoverishing health spending: share pushed below the poverty line by medical bills
  • Effective coverage: hypertension controlled, not merely diagnosed
  • Public-sector health worker density and rural vacancy rate
  • Domestic share of essential medicine and diagnostic production

The two tests

Endogeneity. Active pharmaceutical ingredient and device import dependence; Reliance on foreign-trained clinicians and donor-financed vertical programmes; External share of current health expenditure.

Durability. Is entitlement statutory, or does it lapse with a named scheme?; Coverage retained through a fiscal consolidation year; Primary care staffing retained through a political transition.

Known data defectUHC coverage is a modelled index of tracer indicators, not an observation of care received. Out-of-pocket shares are survey-based and understate forgone care: a household that cannot afford treatment never appears in the spending data.
D3 Work that sustains a life Does a job reproduce a household, or merely register as activity?

What gets measured instead

Unemployment rate; GDP per worker; formal job-creation counts

Why that fails

The unemployment rate excludes anyone who has stopped looking and counts an hour of informal piece work as employment. In economies where most work is informal, it measures almost nothing.

Substance indicators

  • Labour force participation, total and female, age 15 and over
  • Informal employment share of total employment
  • Share of workers with a written contract, paid leave and pension entitlement
  • Labour share of national income
  • Real wage growth less productivity growth
  • Youth not in employment, education or training (NEET)
  • Unpaid care burden in minutes per day, by sex (non-SNA production)

The two tests

Endogeneity. Remittance share of household income; Commodity rent share of exports; export market concentration; Dependence on a single external buyer or single sector for employment.

Durability. Employment retained through a demand shock; Share of jobs that exist without subsidy, protection or a public payroll; Re-employment speed after displacement.

Known data defectLabour force surveys change methodology. India moved from the quinquennial Employment-Unemployment Survey to an annual PLFS in 2017-18, and the treatment of unpaid helpers in household enterprises materially shifts the female participation rate. Series breaks are routinely read as trends.
D4 Security against shocks If income stops tomorrow, does the household survive it?

What gets measured instead

Law and order; homicide rate; rule-of-law perception indices

Why that fails

Order is the precondition of security, not its content. A state can be orderly and still leave every household one illness away from destitution. Perception indices of governance also track the opinions of a narrow expert panel.

Substance indicators

  • Effective social protection coverage (at least one benefit)
  • Pension receipt above statutory retirement age
  • Share able to raise emergency funds within 30 days
  • Depth of health cover: share of the cost borne, not share of people enrolled
  • Moderate or severe food insecurity (FIES)
  • Shock responsiveness: coverage expansion sustained beyond the emergency

The two tests

Endogeneity. Share of protection financed domestically and delivered by statute; Reliance on donor or NGO delivery of core transfers; Whether benefits are contributory entitlements or discretionary transfers.

Durability. Coverage retained after an election; Benefit real value retained through an inflation episode; Portability across employers, sectors and states.

Known data defectEffective coverage counts receipt of at least one benefit, so a country paying a small child grant to many households scores well while leaving unemployment and disability unprotected. Adequacy is not measured.
D5 Fiscal and administrative capacity Can the state tax, count, adjudicate and enforce?

What gets measured instead

Ease of doing business; government effectiveness perception score

Why that fails

Business-friendliness measures the cost of compliance for firms, which is a different object from the capacity of the state to raise revenue, produce statistics and enforce contracts for everyone else.

Substance indicators

  • Tax to GDP, and the direct tax share of total tax
  • Tax effort: actual revenue against estimated capacity frontier
  • Local government own-source revenue as a share of local spending
  • Judicial pendency: stock of cases, and median time to disposal
  • Statistical performance: can the state produce its own indicators?
  • Capital versus subsidy composition of public spending

The two tests

Endogeneity. Revenue dependence on resource rents, aid or a single tax handle; Dependence on external technical assistance to run core surveys; Whether the fiscal base is domestic economic activity or an offshore booking centre.

Durability. Revenue retained through a commodity price fall; Institutional authority vested in offices rather than individuals; Continuity of statistical releases across administrations.

Known data defectStatistical capacity is measured by the World Bank SPI across 51 indicators covering only 14 of 22 framework dimensions; eight dimensions have no established measure at all. The capacity to measure capacity is itself unmeasured.
D6 Household material substance Does the output reach the household, and what does it buy?

What gets measured instead

GDP per capita, at purchasing power parity

Why that fails

Output produced on a territory is not income received by its residents. Repatriated profit, depreciation and profit-shifting all sit between the two. A per-capita average also says nothing about the median household.

Substance indicators

  • Real household consumption per capita
  • Household final consumption as a share of GDP
  • Food share of the household budget (Engel coefficient)
  • Housing cost as a share of disposable income
  • Household debt service ratio
  • Top-decile income share; Gini
  • Profit-shifting-adjusted national income where the gap is material

The two tests

Endogeneity. Remittance share of household consumption; Import dependence of the staple consumption basket; Whether household income derives from domestic production or transferred rent.

Durability. Consumption retained through a currency or terms-of-trade shock; Asset holdings that can be drawn down; Real income retained through a food and fuel price spike.

Known data defectHousehold consumption surveys are infrequent and expensive, and their consumption aggregates are not reconciled to national accounts. Survey-to-accounts gaps of large size are common and rarely explained.

07

What would show this framework is wrong

A framework that cannot be wrong is not a framework. These are the conditions under which this one fails, stated so they can be tested.

The endogeneity proxies are crude

Import dependence, expatriate staffing and remittance shares are observable but blunt. If the axis correlates more with trade openness than with fragility, it is measuring the wrong thing and should be dropped.

Durability is inferred, not observed

No cross-country series measures whether a capability survives a shock, because shocks are irregular. The proxies are structural features: predictive, not dispositive. The test is whether they predict actual capability loss in a subsequent crisis.

Equal domain weighting is arbitrary

Defensible on transparency grounds, arbitrary nonetheless. If the ordering is not robust to plausible reweighting, the instrument is producing a preference artefact.

The penalty embeds a value judgement

That a failed domain cannot be offset is arguable. The γ path exists so a reader who disagrees can see what the disagreement costs.

It is a diagnostic, not a forecast

Nothing here predicts growth, crisis or regime change. Any use in that mode would be unsupported by the instrument.

The indicator degrades when targeted

Goodhart's mechanism applies directly. If allocation depends on a durability score, durability becomes something to perform. Prefer indicators that are expensive to manipulate, and keep the underlying series visible.
